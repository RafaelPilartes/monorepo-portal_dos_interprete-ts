# Step H2 — Accounts, roles, app switching, organisations and invites, suspension, consent

> Executed inline in continuous mode; independent review at the end of the step.

**Goal:** a person registers (with consent to the published legal documents), confirms the email in the local mail server, chooses a role in onboarding, switches between the requester and interpreter apps without signing in again, creates an organisation, invites by email, and the invited person accepts. The team lists and suspends or reactivates accounts and organisations. A new legal version forces acceptance everywhere.

**Spec:** `docs/plan/specs/accounts-and-organizations.md` (R-ACC-01..06, R-ORG-01..05, criteria 1–7, criterion 5 only for the directory); `docs/plan/specs/personal-data.md` R-PRIV-01/02 (criteria 1–2); `docs/plan/data-model.md` §1; `docs/plan/api.md` §4, §5, §12 (suspend/reactivate).

## Decisions taken here (claude-code-rules.md §3)
- **Migration `0003-accounts.sql`**: `identity.requester_profiles`, `identity.interpreter_profiles` (all columns of data-model §1; H4 fills them in), `identity.organizations`, `identity.organization_members` (one active owner: unique partial index), `identity.organization_invites` (hash of the token only), `identity.consents` (append-only). `interpreter_payout_details` (H10) and `account_deletion_requests` (H14) wait for their steps.
- **Directory exit (criterion 5, H2 part):** `directory.listed_interpreters` is created now as a minimal view (interpreters whose account is active); H4 redefines it with profile completeness. Suspension removes the interpreter because the view follows the account status — tested with a forced error in the middle of `identity.suspend_account` (nothing changes).
- **Terms (R-PRIV-02):** an account has documents to accept when, for a kind with a published version, its latest published version has no row in `consents` for the account. `auth_helper.account_id()` returns NULL then (RLS denies), `auth_helper.account_status()` returns `terms_required`. The bootstrapped super_admin accepts on the screen like everyone else.
- **Registration (`POST /auth/register`)** calls the Auth server's sign-up from `apps/api/src/integrations/auth.ts` (the confirmation email goes through the Auth server to the local mail server), with `full_name` and `intended_role` in the user metadata and `emailRedirectTo` = `<app of the intended role>/verify-email`. `EMAIL_IN_USE` is decided by a database lookup before calling the Auth server. Consents are recorded right after, in one transaction; if that fails the account exists without consent and the terms screen asks for it at the first sign-in (nothing is lost).
- **Invite links** go to `requester-web` (`/invite/:token`); the token is generated in SQL (`extensions.gen_random_bytes`), only its sha256 is stored, and it travels in the `org_invite` email payload. A public `GET /invites/preview?token=` shows organisation, role and the masked email before accepting (the spec's screen needs it; api.md lists only the POST — AD-030).
- **Notices of the step:** `org_invite` (E), `org_invite_accepted` (P), `account_suspended` (E), `terms_updated` (E, on publishing a new legal version). Each has a test that makes it fire. `account_suspended_counterparty` needs requests (H8).
- **anonymize_account skeleton:** `identity.anonymize_account(account)` covers `profiles`, `requester_profiles`, `interpreter_profiles`, pending invites sent to the email, memberships; the Auth part (email swap, sessions) is H14's, with the screen.
- **Front end:** `packages/auth` gets Register (real), VerifyEmail, ResetPassword, AcceptTerms, Suspended, the post-login destination (R-ACC-04) and `RequireRole` with onboarding fallback; the three panel apps mount them. requester-web: onboarding (individual / organisation), `/organization`, `/organization/members`, `/invite/:token`, the "A pedir como" context. interpreter-web: onboarding. admin-web: `/requesters`, `/requesters/:id`, `/organizations`, `/organizations/:id`, `/interpreters`, `/interpreters/:id` paginated on the server, suspend/reactivate. The dashboard header shows the real name, the role switch and sign-out. The touched panel screens are refined with the impeccable skill (Rafael, 2026-10-26).

## Review Focus
1. Accepting an invite with another account's session → `INVITE_EMAIL_MISMATCH`; two accepts of the same invite → one membership.
2. Removing, demoting or leaving as the only owner → `LAST_OWNER`; concurrent transfers never leave zero or two owners.
3. A suspended account: every API route `ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED`, zero rows by RLS, the app shows "Conta suspensa".
4. `returnTo` with an external URL is ignored after login and after email confirmation.
5. A new legal version: every route except `/me` and `/me/accept-terms` answers `TERMS_ACCEPTANCE_REQUIRED`; direct reads return nothing until accepted.
