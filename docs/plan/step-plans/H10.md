# Step H10 — Payments, proofs, refunds, payouts and receipts

> Executed inline in continuous mode. Independent review at the end of the step.

**Goal:** every payment through the Portal is an intent paid by bank transfer with a proof checked by finance (ADR-005); refunds and payouts are records finance pays by hand; receipts are numbered without gaps and are not invoices (D-36).

**Spec:** `docs/plan/specs/payments.md` (R-PAY-01..13, criteria 1–7); D-30..D-37; data-model.md §6. The subjects (managed request H11, verification H12, paid events H17) arrive later: here the flow is seen with an intent created by fixture (MASTER-PLAN H10).

## Decisions (AD-039)
- Migration `0012-payments.sql`: `billing.payment_intents` (one live per subject), `payment_proofs` (one pending), `refunds`, `payouts`, `receipts` (immutable), `identity.interpreter_payout_details`.
- `99c-billing.sql`: `split_amount` (the only tax computation), `managed_interpreter_share`, `create_intent`, `submit_proof` (the file must be the one signed for the intent and present in storage), `review_proof`, `expire_intents` (+ 12 h warning), `cancel_intent_for_subject`, `create_refund` (intent locked), `create_manual_refund`, `mark_refund_paid`, `queue_payout`, `mark_payout_paid`, `cancel_payout`, `issue_receipt` (`util.next_code('RC')` inside the transaction), `identity.save_payout_details`; `billing.on_subject_event` is the hook H11/H12/H17 fill.
- The receipt PDF is written by the API by hand (one page of text), no new dependency.

## Tasks
1. Database and SQL assertions `0012-payments.sql` (criteria 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, the proof flow, organisations, expiry, payouts).
