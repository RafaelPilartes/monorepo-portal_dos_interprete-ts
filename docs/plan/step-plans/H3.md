# Step H3 — Catalogs (languages, specialties, services) and the real /services page

> Executed inline in continuous mode; independent review at the end of the step.

**Goal:** the team manages languages, specialties and services in admin-web; an item that was used is deactivated, never deleted; a service's pricing mode never changes after use; the public `/services` page reads the active services from the database.

**Spec:** `docs/plan/specs/catalogs.md` (R-CAT-01, R-CAT-02, criteria 1–4); `docs/plan/data-model.md` §2; `docs/plan/api.md` §11.

## Decisions taken here (claude-code-rules.md §3)
- **Migration `0004-catalogs.sql`:** `catalog.languages`, `catalog.specialties`, `catalog.services`, and — pulled forward from H4 so "used" exists and criteria 1–2 are testable (MASTER-PLAN, "O que quebra a sequência" 1) — `directory.interpreter_languages`, `directory.interpreter_specialties`, `directory.interpreter_services` with their columns of data-model §3. H4 adds their policies, grants for the owner's writes and the screens.
- **Usage in one place:** `catalog.usage_count(kind, id)` counts the rows that use an item; each later step that references a catalog (requests H5, opportunities H17) adds its table there. `catalog.guard_delete` (BEFORE DELETE) and `catalog.guard_service_pricing_mode` (BEFORE UPDATE) call it. Delete refused → `IN_USE`; pricing mode change refused → `VALIDATION_FAILED` on `pricing_mode` ("O modo de preço não muda depois de usado. Cria um serviço novo e desactiva este.").
- **`icon`** holds a key (`education`, `health`, …), not an emoji: the site maps it to its image, admin to an icon.
- **Site `/services`:** names, descriptions, price and order from the database; the images, examples and the detail modal's long text stay as site content keyed by slug in `components/serviceContent.ts` (marketing copy, not data); a service without content shows only the database fields.
- `catalog.quote_service` (R-REQ-02) belongs to H5 with the requests.

## Review Focus
1. Deleting a used language/specialty/service → `IN_USE`, also through the API.
2. Changing `pricing_mode` of a used service fails; changing the price does not.
3. Inactive items never reach anonymous readers or new choices; the team sees them.
4. Duplicate code/slug → error on the field in Portuguese.
5. `/services` with zero active services shows an honest empty state.
