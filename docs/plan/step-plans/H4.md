# Step H4 — Interpreter profile, availability, content, directory and favorites

> Executed inline in continuous mode, in two parts: H4a (database, API, tests) and H4b (screens). Independent review at the end of the step.

**Goal:** the interpreter fills in a real profile (overview, background, languages, specialties, services with prices, contacts, content, availability); a complete profile of an active account appears in the directory; visitors see the public listing and are sent to login for the full profile; accounts see the full profile and keep favorites; the team removes content with a reason.

**Spec:** `docs/plan/specs/interpreter-profile-and-directory.md` (R-DIR-01..10, criteria 1–6); `docs/plan/data-model.md` §3; `docs/plan/api.md` §12, §16.

## Decisions taken here (claude-code-rules.md §3; recorded in AD-033)
- **Migration `0006-directory.sql`:** availability (rules and blocked days), education, experiences, certifications, media items, articles, favorites. 0004 already has the language, specialty and service tables.
- **R-DIR-01 written once, in a view** (`directory.profile_status`, missing items as keys). `directory.is_profile_complete`/`profile_missing` read it for the API; `directory.my_profile_status` gives the owner its own list; `directory.listed_interpreters` and `directory.public_listing` join it. The foundation rule "authenticated runs only `auth_helper`, anon runs nothing" stays: the RPC of api.md §16 becomes the view `my_profile_status`.
- **R-DIR-04 in one helper:** `auth_helper.can_read_interpreter` (owner, team, or any active account when listed), used by every policy of the profile and its parts.
- **Writes:** the owner writes its rows directly (column grants, RLS). The foundation test lists the new column grants and the own-row DELETE grants.
- **Content removal:** `POST /admin/content/{videos,articles}/:id/remove` → `directory.remove_content` (audit `content.removed`, notification `content_removed` with email).
- **Not in H4:** `directory.is_eligible_for` (R-DIR-09, criterion 4) needs `booking.requests` → H5. Ratings (R-DIR-05), late cancellations (R-DIR-06) and the badge come with their tables (H8/H9, H12); `public_listing` carries `is_verified = false`, `rating_average = NULL`, `rating_count = 0` until then. "Denunciar" needs the reports of the community steps; "Pedir este intérprete" opens the request wizard of H5.

## Tasks
1. H4a — migration, functions, views, policies, grants; SQL assertions `0006-directory.sql` (criteria 1, 2, 3, 5); H2 assertions updated (the directory now needs a complete profile); route and email template of the removal with its API test; types regenerated.
2. H4b — data layer (repositories, view models) and screens: interpreter-web `/profile` and sub-routes, `/availability`; `packages/features` public profile, `/videos`, `/articles`; site-web `/interpreters`; requester-web `/interpreters`, `/favorites`. Mocks deleted.
3. Playwright: the interpreter completes the profile → appears on the site → the visitor is sent to login → back to the profile (criterion 6).
